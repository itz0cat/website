How It Works

How CatOrder Works (Autonomous Macro Routine)

Built on a multi-stage automated state machine that safely executes orders with anti-spam pacing and zero manual interaction.

1

Balance Snapshotting & Pre-Cycle Check (START)

CatOrder parses your player balance via /bal to establish a baseline. It inspects your inventory: if you already hold 2,304 or more bones, it immediately skips the purchase stage to save money and moves straight to fulfilling active orders.

2

Automated Bulk Shop Routine (BUY_BONES)

Sends /shop with an enforced 2.1s anti-spam cooldown, navigates through the Mob Drops category, selects Bone, opens the Chest bulk purchase window, and buys full stacks (36x bones) until your inventory is packed, then cleanly closes the GUI.

3

Sign Packet Interception & Search (ORDER_SEARCH)

Sends /order, clicks the search compass, and intercepts the server's SignEditorOpenS2CPacket. Instead of opening a manual screen, CatOrder automatically submits an UpdateSignC2SPacket with "bone" on line 1, immediately returning live player buy orders.

4

Smart Sorting & Shift-Click Delivery (DELIVER_BONES)

Configures order sorting to "Most Money Per Item" (slot 47), selects the highest paying active bone order, shift-clicks your bones into the delivery container, and clicks the confirm button (slot 16). If an order cancels or expires, it safely clicks slot 10 to retrieve all items without losses.

5

Profit Verification & Repeat Loop (LOG_PROFIT)

Runs up to 12 consecutive rounds until your bone stock is fully cleared. CatOrder queries /bal, calculates your exact cycle net profit and percentage growth, logs the metrics to your chat, and seamlessly begins the next round.
