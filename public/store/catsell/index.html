How CatSell Works (State Machine Architecture)

Built on a deterministic 5-stage state machine that operates cleanly on the client side without triggering anti-cheat heuristics.

1

Rotation-Locking & Startup (START)

When you run /catsell start, the mod verifies your license key with CatCore, then automatically snapshots your player's exact look angles (Yaw and Pitch). This locks your crosshair dead-center onto the targeted chest block for every repetition.

2

Autonomous Container Access (OPEN_CHEST)

CatSell snaps your camera back to the saved look angles and safely fires a block interaction packet to right-click the chest. It includes a built-in 10-second watchdog timer to handle lag or block placement delays.

3

Empty-Slot Waiting & Scanner (CHECK)

While keeping the chest GUI open, CatSell continuously scans every container slot. If any slot is still empty (e.g. your automatic hopper or farm is still filling it), CatSell patiently waits and rescans every 5 seconds until zero empty slots remain. No wasted trips or half-empty inventories.

4

High-Speed Shift-Click Looting (LOOT)

Once the container is 100% full, CatSell rapid-fires shift-clicks with configurable tick delays (default 2 ticks) to fill your player inventory. As soon as your inventory is full or the chest is exhausted, it cleanly closes the GUI.

5

Automated Selling & Continuous Loop (SELL)

CatSell immediately sends /sellgui, transfers all looted items into the sell window, and closes it. If your server uses instant sell commands, it automatically falls back to /sell. Once sold, it seamlessly restarts at Step 2 for 24/7 autonomous farming.

In-Game Commands & Controls

Simple, intuitive commands right inside your Minecraft chat.

Command Description
/catsell key <license_key> Activates your license key and cryptographically binds your device HWID.
/catsell start Aims at the chest block in front of you, locks rotation, and begins the auto-sell loop.
/catsell stop Instant kill switch to immediately stop all looting, close open GUIs, and release controls.
/catsell status Displays your license status, current state, locked yaw/pitch, and target chest info.
