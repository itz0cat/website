Refund & Replacement Policy

Last updated: September 2026
Need help with your key? Our support staff is ready to help resolve activation issues, HWID transfers, or key replacements 24/7 via Discord Tickets.

1. Nature of Digital Goods

Cat Game provides non-tangible, irrevocable digital goods in the form of cryptographic software license keys and companion mod access. Once a key has been generated and delivered to your Discord ticket or direct message, the purchase is generally considered final.

2. Replacement Eligibility

We are committed to delivering 100% working licenses. You are eligible for an immediate, hassle-free replacement key or troubleshooting assistance under the following conditions:

  • Defective or Invalid Key: If your key fails to authenticate upon delivery due to a backend generation issue.
  • HWID Lockout: If you upgraded your PC hardware and require an authorized device reset.
  • Unresolved Technical Conflict: If the mod crashes in your environment and our support team cannot provide a solution within 48 hours.

3. Refund Eligibility & Criteria

Refunds may be evaluated on a case-by-case basis under the following limited conditions:

  • The refund request is submitted via a Discord ticket within 24 hours of the initial purchase.
  • The license key has not been actively utilized for multiple hours of server gameplay or transferred across unauthorized devices.
  • A technical problem on our cloud infrastructure prevents solver operation and cannot be rectified in a reasonable timeframe.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be granted under the following circumstances:

  • Server-side bans or disciplinary actions enacted by third-party server administrators.
  • Buyer's remorse after the key has been validated and utilized.
  • Violation of our Terms of Service (e.g., unauthorized key sharing, leaking, or abuse).

5. How to Submit a Request

To request a key replacement or refund, join our Discord server at discord.gg/Ga6y7rzd5u, open a support ticket with the billing/order role, and provide your license key and transaction receipt.
