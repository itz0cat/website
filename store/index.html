What Cat Game Does

Engineered specifically for Minecraft Fabric 1.21.x to detect and win every chat game cleanly.

Fill-In-The-Blank Matcher

Indexes 2,466+ official Minecraft item, block, and mob registry names. Instantly decodes patterns like _rown Co_cret_ ___d__ into Brown Concrete Powder.

Instant Anagram Unscrambler

High-speed letter-frequency algorithm solves scrambled words (e.g. Onraeg loWo → Orange Wool) in single-digit milliseconds.

Complex Equation Solver

Handles negative numbers, nested operator precedence, and arithmetic equations with 100% precision before human players can calculate them.

Predictive Cooldown Radar

Constantly monitors server timing patterns and gives you a 20-second warning in your Action Bar so you never get caught on chat cooldown when a game drops.

Human Jitter Anti-Cheat

Engineered with micro-delays and randomized typing jitter to keep your answers within natural human timing windows and protect against detection.

SHA-256 HWID Security

Hardware IDs are cryptographically hashed locally before communicating with our cloud API. Zero leaked credentials and zero server-side impersonation.
